Our Story

The Art of Restraint

The Founder

Claire Dubois

After a decade at the helm of some of Paris's most prestigious fashion houses, Claire Dubois founded MAISON NOIR in 2019 with a singular vision: to create clothing that whispers rather than shouts.

"I wanted to build a brand that respects both the wearer and the craft. Every seam, every cut, every fabric choice is deliberate. We don't follow trends — we create wardrobes that transcend them."

"Elegance is the art of knowing what to leave out."

Our Values

What We Stand For

01

Craftsmanship

Every piece is meticulously crafted by skilled artisans in our Parisian atelier. We believe in the human touch — no shortcuts, no compromises.

02

Sustainability

We source only ethically produced, natural fabrics. Our supply chain is transparent, and we are committed to reducing our environmental footprint every season.

03

Timelessness

We design for longevity, not fleeting trends. Our pieces are meant to be worn season after season, growing more beautiful with time.
